THE DOCTRINE // SECTION 02

Tested Under Pressure

Leadership is not only tested by what happens. It is tested by how you respond.

// OPERATIONAL PHILOSOPHY
“Leadership is not only tested by what happens. It is tested by how you respond.”

Leadership often requires decisions to be made with incomplete information, competing priorities and little time to consider the alternatives. The ability to remain composed, communicate clearly and bring people together becomes particularly important when familiar ways of working no longer apply.

Operation Forge X is designed to explore these qualities through experience. Across three days, participants enter a structured, military-inspired environment led by retired officers and experienced faculty. Physical activities, team missions and leadership sessions create opportunities to observe how individuals and groups respond to unfamiliar situations.

THE MISSION

Develop your ability to respond under pressure, strengthen the way you work with others, and leave with a clearer understanding of your own approach to leadership.

Build the composure and psychological readiness to lead through adversity, fatigue, and sudden operational disruptions without losing your team.

DECLASSIFIED INTEL // SECTION 09

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential operational details regarding eligibility, physical readiness, venue, and fee structures.

Operation Forge X is designed for business leaders, founders, entrepreneurs, senior professionals and emerging leaders who want to strengthen their leadership capabilities. It is suited to individuals and teams willing to challenge themselves, collaborate with others and learn through experience.
No prior military experience is intended to be necessary. The programme is built around guided, military-led experiences that introduce participants to leadership principles through practical challenges. Specific outdoor experience requirements, if any, will be shared before registration.
Operation Forge X includes physically active, outdoor challenges and team-based missions. Participants should be prepared to step outside their comfort zone and take an active role throughout the experience. Detailed fitness and medical requirements will be communicated before registration.
The programme features retired officers from the Indian Army, Air Force and Navy, bringing military leadership perspectives to the experience. Participants will engage with experienced faculty through leadership sessions, field challenges and guided debriefs.
The expedition takes place from 27–30 November 2026 at Hyatt Regency Dehradun Resort and Spa. The four-day, three-night experience combines outdoor challenges and leadership sessions with accommodation and scheduled programme experiences.
The programme fee is structured around two accommodation options: Buddy Pair at ₹80,000 + GST per person and Private Quarters at ₹1,10,000 + GST per person. The package is planned to include accommodation, meals, leadership sessions, field challenges, programme kit and scheduled evening experiences.
Operation Forge X is a civilian leadership experience led by retired military personnel. It is not an official programme of, or affiliated with, the Indian Army, Indian Air Force or Indian Navy.
